Soggy Moggy · L3 Water Variants

Each canvas is 480×320. Sky gradient is static (for preview only). Water is the animated effect. Same time base for fair comparison.

Variant A — Retro Pixel Water

Horizontal depth bands + smooth sine surface + drifting highlight lines. Fits the pixel-art aesthetic.

Variant B — Overlapping Smooth Sines

Three smooth per-pixel sine waves stacked with alpha blending. Natural depth through transparency.

Variant C — Smooth Wave + Shimmer

Single clean sine surface with drifting white highlight streaks. Minimal, elegant, calm.

Variant D — Stormy Sea

Compound sine (two summed) + foam caps on crests + depth gradient. Lively without electricity vibe.
